How to play Blandis

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Blandis is a free physics puzzle game that runs in your browser, on mobile and desktop, with nothing to install. Pick a soft numbered blob, launch it into the jar and merge equal numbers all the way up to 2048. Every blob is a squishy ring that deforms to fill the gaps around it.

Rules

  1. Up to 3 blobs wait in the tray at the top. Press the one you want to use.
  2. Drag toward where it should go (an arrow shows the shot) and release. The further you drag, the harder it flies.
  3. A short tap drops it gently; a hard shot pushes others and squeezes into gaps.
  4. Two equal numbers that touch merge into their double: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8… up to 2048.
  5. Back-to-back merges make a combo and more points.
  6. If any blob stays above the dashed line for 3 seconds, the game ends.

Once you reach 256, blobs of 64 join the mix, so the jar gets tighter as you go.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: press a blob in the tray, drag to aim and release to launch. To cancel, release it back on the blob or aim above the danger line.
  • Keyboard: Esc or P pauses the game; C cancels the launch.

Game modes

Each mode adds one rule on top of the classic game and has its own best scores and its own global leaderboard.

  • Classic: merge as long as you can. Don't let it overflow.
  • Time attack: score as much as you can in 2 minutes.
  • Speedrun: tray full? Launch within 3 seconds or you lose.
  • Sprint 512: make a 512 as fast as you can.
  • 40 shots: only 40 launches, so make every one count.

Achievements and color styles

There are more than 70 achievements to collect: merges, score, combos, skill shots, survival, lifetime stats and challenges for each game mode. Seven of them unlock color styles for the game: Cozy, Neon, Cloud, Ocean, Caramel, Night and Rainbow. You can also set your own background image inside the jar from Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Is Blandis free?

Yes. It is a personal, non-commercial project with no ads and nothing to buy.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Blandis runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Is it like 2048?

It uses the same doubling (2 + 2 = 4, up to 2048), but instead of sliding tiles on a grid you launch soft blobs into a jar, and real soft-body physics decides where they land and what they touch.

Which languages is it available in?

English and Spanish. You can switch in Settings → Language.

Where is my progress saved?

Your best scores, achievements and settings are saved in your own browser, on your device. Clearing the site's data deletes them.

How does the global leaderboard work?

After a game you can choose a nickname to join the public leaderboard of that game mode. It is optional; see the Terms and the Privacy policy.
