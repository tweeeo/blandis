# Blandis: a squishy physics merge puzzle

> Pick, launch and squish soft numbered blobs so equal numbers merge up to 2048. A free physics puzzle game for mobile and desktop browsers: no install, no ads.

Blandis is a free puzzle game you play right here in your browser, on a phone, a tablet or a computer. You launch soft numbered blobs into a jar, and when two equal numbers touch they merge into their double: 2 and 2 make 4, 4 and 4 make 8, and so on all the way up to 2048. There is nothing to install, no account to create and no ads.

## Like 2048, but with real physics

The doubling rule will feel familiar if you have played 2048, but instead of sliding tiles across a grid you aim and throw. Every blob is a squishy ring that squashes against the glass, bulges into gaps and gets pushed around by its neighbours. A gentle tap lets a blob slide into place; a hard shot can shove a pile aside and wedge into a space that looked too small. Because the physics decides where everything settles, no two rounds look the same, and good positioning matters as much as good merges.

## How a round works

Up to three blobs wait in a tray above the jar. Pick one, drag to aim and let go. Chain merges back to back to build a combo and score more points. As the numbers grow the jar fills up, and once you reach 256, blobs of 64 start to appear, so space gets tight quickly. If any blob stays above the dashed line at the top for three seconds, the round is over.

## Five ways to play

- **Classic:** the endless original. Keep merging and don't let the jar overflow.
- **Time attack:** two minutes on the clock to score as many points as you can.
- **Speedrun:** when the tray is full you have three seconds to launch, so think fast.
- **Sprint 512:** a race to build a single 512 in the shortest time.
- **40 shots:** forty launches and not one more, so make each throw count.

Every mode keeps its own best scores and has its own global leaderboard, so you can chase a different record in each.

## Achievements, styles and the global top

More than 70 achievements reward big merges, long combos, clever bank shots, close calls and milestones in every mode. Seven of them unlock new color styles for the blobs and the jar, from soft pastels to neon, and you can even put a picture of your own inside the jar. After a round you can add a nickname to the public top 10 of that mode. It's optional: otherwise your scores, achievements and settings stay in your own browser.

## Play anywhere

Blandis works with a mouse, a touchscreen or a keyboard, in English and Spanish, and runs smoothly on modest phones because the physics steps at a fixed rate. It is a personal, non-commercial project made by Joel, and the source code is on GitHub.
