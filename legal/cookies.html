Cookie policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Summary

Blandis does not use cookies. It has no analytics, advertising, social media or tracking technologies.

It uses one entry in your browser's local storage to save your game on your own device. This is strictly necessary for features you ask for (keeping your progress and settings), so no consent banner is shown.

2. What is stored on your device

NameTypeProviderPurposeDuration
blandis-v2 Local storage Blandis (first party) Saves your best score, recent scores, achievements, statistics and settings (style, sound, language). If you join the global leaderboard, it also keeps your nickname and a random player identifier so that your entry can be updated or deleted. Until you clear your browser's site data

Nothing is written until you change a setting or finish a game, and the player identifier is only created when you join the global leaderboard.

Category: strictly necessary / preferences chosen by the user. Under Article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE and the ePrivacy Directive, storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service the user has explicitly requested does not require consent.

This data is not used to track you. It stays on your device, except when you take part in the leaderboard: then your nickname, player identifier, score, game duration and number of merges are sent with each game, as explained in the Privacy policy.

3. Third parties

The game does not load content from third parties: fonts and all other files are served with the game itself. When this policy was last updated, the responses of the website host (GitHub Pages) and of the leaderboard server (Cloudflare Workers) did not set any cookies.

4. Optional technologies

There are none. If any are added in the future (for example, analytics), they will only be used after you give consent, and this policy will be updated.

5. How to delete it

You can delete the stored data at any time from your browser's settings (site data or storage for this website). Doing so resets your progress, achievements and settings. If you take part in the leaderboard, use Settings → Delete my global score in the game before clearing your browser data; afterwards your entry can no longer be linked to you and will only be removed automatically after 12 months without new games.
