Global leaderboard terms

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Scope

Blandis is a free browser game provided by Joel. Playing it does not require an account or accepting these terms.

These terms apply only if you choose to join the optional global leaderboard. Before your first score is sent, the game asks you to confirm that you are 14 or older and that you accept these terms and the Privacy policy. You can leave at any time by deleting your global score in Settings.

2. Taking part

  • The leaderboard is only for players aged 14 or over.
  • After you join, each finished game is sent automatically with your nickname, until you clear your name or delete your global score in Settings.
  • Only your best score is kept. The leaderboard shows the top 10 entries with nickname, score and game duration.
  • Entries are deleted automatically 12 months after the last game sent.

Nicknames

Nicknames may contain up to 16 letters, numbers, spaces, dots, hyphens and underscores. Do not use a nickname that:

  • is your real full name or contains personal data (yours or anyone else's);
  • impersonates another person, brand or organisation;
  • is offensive, discriminatory, sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate;
  • advertises anything.

3. Fair play

Scores must come from a normal game played by a person. The following are not allowed:

  • modifying the game, its memory or its saved data to change scores, times or results;
  • sending, forging or replaying requests to the leaderboard server outside the game;
  • using bots, scripts, macros or other automation to play;
  • exploiting bugs to obtain scores that could not be achieved normally;
  • creating several entries to flood or manipulate the leaderboard;
  • attacking, overloading or trying to gain unauthorised access to the game or its servers.

4. Moderation

To keep the leaderboard fair and safe:

  • scores that a real game cannot produce are rejected automatically;
  • entries with fraudulent or implausible scores, or with nicknames that break these terms, may be removed;
  • submissions from sources used for abuse may be blocked;
  • the leaderboard may be reset, for example after changes to the game rules.

To report a nickname or ask about an entry that was removed, write to ice.dev1234@gmail.com.
